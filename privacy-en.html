TapanGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Also in: 한국어 · Español

TapanGo does not collect your data. There is no account and no TapanGo server. Your timeline stays on your phone.

Who this is from

TapanGo is a time-tracking app. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ejson323@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

Every tap, the names and notes you give blocks, your categories, your goals and your settings are kept in a database on your phone. The home-screen widget on the same phone reads and writes the same data. None of it is sent to us.

What leaves your phone

Nothing leaves by itself. If you use Export, the file is made on your phone and goes only where you choose to send it. The "Talk to an AI" file is made only when you choose to share it, and you choose the app that receives it; what that app does with the file is governed by its own policy. TapanGo sends nothing itself.

Purchases

TapanGo Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. On launch the app asks the store whether Plus is owned. The store handles the purchase; TapanGo never receives your payment details or your store account.

Notifications

The evening reminder is scheduled on your phone. It uses no server and sends no data.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. TapanGo has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and does not track you across apps or websites.

Your phone's own backup

If you use iCloud backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android, your phone may keep a copy of TapanGo's data in that backup. That copy is held by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes your timeline, categories and goals from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes its data too. A backup of your phone made by Apple or Google may still hold an older copy until you delete that backup, and files you exported stay where you saved them.

Children

Because TapanGo collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you think otherwise, write to us and we will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published here with a new date, and the Privacy page in the app will be updated in the same release.

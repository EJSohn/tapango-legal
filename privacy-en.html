TapanGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Also in: 한국어 · Español

Your timeline stays on your device. App usage statistics are optional and are sent to Google Analytics only with your permission.

Who this is from

TapanGo is a time-tracking app. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to tapango.kr@gmail.com.

Your timeline stays on this device

Every tap, every name you give a block and every correction is kept in a database on this phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no TapanGo server holding a copy. If the phone is lost, so is the record — unless your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) is switched on, which may keep a copy of TapanGo's data under Apple's or Google's terms. Exporting a backup file is part of TapanGo Plus.

Files you send to an AI app are your choice

The Talk to an AI file is made only when you choose to share it, and you choose the app that receives it. TapanGo does not send this file itself. What that app does with the file is up to its own policy.

Purchases

TapanGo Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. On launch the app asks the store whether Plus is owned. The store handles the purchase; TapanGo never receives your payment details or your store account.

Notifications

The evening reminder is scheduled on your phone. It uses no server and sends no data.

Optional usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase only after you allow App usage statistics. Google LLC and its service providers process usage events and SDK information to help us understand activity and improve features. Processing may take place in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Collection occurs over the network while allowed; declining does not limit app features. You can withdraw in Settings at any time. We do not use this information for advertising or link it to an app account. If you allow App usage statistics, Firebase Analytics sends screen visits, feature usage, Plus status, and SDK information such as app version, device information, installation identifiers, and approximate region to Google Analytics. Category names, labels, memo contents, record IDs, and exact activity times are not sent by our events. Advertising identifiers, Google signals, and advertising personalization are disabled. Collection is off before permission and after you decline or turn it off in Settings. Event-level retention is set to two months; aggregated reports may remain longer. App Store or Google Play handles purchases; TapanGo does not receive payment details or your store account. The SDK may also collect store purchase events automatically.

Your phone's own backup

If you use iCloud backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android, your phone may keep a copy of TapanGo's data in that backup. That copy is held by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us.

Deleting means deleting

Delete everything removes your timeline, categories, and goals from this device, resets your usage-statistics choice, and stops collection. On iOS it also resets the SDK’s local analytics state. This does not delete statistics already sent to Google. A phone backup made by Apple or Google may still hold an older copy, and exported files stay where you saved them. For questions about previously sent statistics, contact tapango.kr@gmail.com. Without an account we may be unable to identify an individual’s analytics records. On a web preview, clearing site data is needed to fully clear browser analytics identifiers.

Children

For questions about children’s data or consent, contact tapango.kr@gmail.com. Recorded category names, labels, and memo contents are not included in usage-statistics events.

Your rights

Contact tapango.kr@gmail.com for questions or requests concerning access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal. We have no app accounts, so we may not be able to identify analytics records belonging to an individual. Local deletion and SDK reset do not erase data already processed by Google. Requests are handled according to applicable law and the available identifiers; we do not promise deletion we cannot verify.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published here with a new date, and the Privacy page in the app will be updated in the same release.
